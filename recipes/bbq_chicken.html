BBQ Chicken


Description

BBQ Chicken is a delicious dish that features tender chicken coated in a smoky barbecue sauce. It's perfect for grilling and is a favorite at summer cookouts and family gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  3. Brush the chicken with olive oil.
  4. Place the chicken on the grill and cook for 6-8 minutes per side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  5. Brush the chicken with BBQ sauce during the last few minutes of cooking.
